# Development Roadmap

**Status:** Proposed sequence and gates.

## Stage 0 — Specification freeze
Define N1 electrical interface and channel transfer functions, supply/signals, acceptance criteria and feature traceability. **Exit:** reviewed schematic plan and test protocol.

## Stage 1 — N1 single neuron
Four dendrites, six soma channels, membrane, threshold, spike circuit, refractory, calcium-like/adaptation states and axon branches. **Exit:** N1 acceptance tests pass with archived measurements.

## Stage 2 — Synapse and small pair
Excitatory/inhibitory synapses, configurable conductance, short-term dynamics and pair timing experiments. **Exit:** stable two-to-four neuron interaction and measured update response.

## Stage 3 — 16-neuron micro-connectome
Recurrent topology, regional interfaces, local modulation and optional DSAG experiment. **Exit:** reproducible recurrent behaviour, stability and energy documented.

## Stage 4 — Sensory front ends
Optical, auditory, touch, proximity, inertial and proprioceptive channels. **Exit:** calibrated inputs produce distinguishable connectome responses.

## Stage 5 — 64/128-neuron expansion
Associative/spatial populations, memory/eligibility, predictive and affective modulation. **Exit:** power, thermal, noise and stability limits met.

## Stage 6 — 512-neuron target
Sparse regional network, scalable interconnect/backplane, calibration and fault isolation. **Exit:** full network operates within measured limits; this is a target, not a guarantee.

## Stage 7 — Motor substrate
Analogue planning/execution, wheel feedback and independent emergency stop. **Exit:** restrained low-speed tests and stopping performance documented.

## Stage 8 — Mobile integration
Six-wheel chassis, protected battery/enclosure, sensors and bumpers. **Exit:** controlled environment tests with repeatable sensorimotor responses.

## Stage 9 — Advanced research
Memristive synapses, custom analogue ICs, larger connectomes, long-term physical memory and advanced sensory arrays. Each capability requires a dedicated experiment and evidence.

A failed gate creates an issue and corrective experiment; it does not justify silently deleting a requirement.
