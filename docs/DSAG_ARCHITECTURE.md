# DSAG — Dual-Soma Axonal Graft Research Architecture

**Status:** Advanced proposed research pathway; not a validated circuit or biological claim.

Retain DSAG as an additional pathway while preserving the standard six-channel soma neuron.

## Proposed structure

- Soma A: independently maintained processing state.
- Soma B: independently maintained processing state.
- Bidirectional coupling: measurable influence in both directions.
- Axonal-dendritic graft: configurable delayed feedback pathway.
- Independent thresholds and refractory states unless testing supports a different design.

The provisional allocation of A to rapid/local processing and B to context/temporal integration is a hypothesis to test.

## Constraints

Both somata retain explicit channel definitions. No hidden digital controller combines their outputs. Coupling is bounded and stable, feedback delay/gain adjustable, each soma/graft exposes probe points, and failure of one path must not destructively saturate the other.

## Experiments

Test each soma in isolation; one-way A-to-B and B-to-A; bidirectional stability; delayed graft response; coupling modulation; plasticity with/without graft; and comparison against a single-soma control. Report oscillation, saturation, recovery, timing and energy. Do not claim improved cognition without task-level comparative evidence.
