# Initial Experimental Protocols

These are protocol outlines. Define numerical acceptance thresholds before collecting data.

1. **Four-compartment isolation:** stimulate D1–D4 separately and together; record compartment and soma outputs; quantify cross-coupling, gain and time response.
2. **Six-channel separation:** inject each S1–S6 independently, then combinations; measure transfer, coupling, saturation and recovery.
3. **Spike/refractory:** sweep input amplitude and pulse timing; measure threshold, waveform, pulse width, refractory interval and recovery.
4. **Calcium-like adaptation:** apply trains with varying frequency/duration; record slow-state decay, saturation and influence on threshold/gain.
5. **Synaptic polarity/conductance:** measure E/I response into defined loads over conductance and pulse sweeps.
6. **Pairwise timing plasticity:** vary pre/post interval both orders; measure conductance change, repeatability and drift.
7. **Neuromodulatory influence:** apply each modulation signal alone and in combinations; measure gain, threshold, time constant and plasticity.
8. **Connectome stability:** increase recurrent coupling gradually; measure oscillation, saturation, recovery and power.
9. **Sensory discrimination:** apply controlled optical, acoustic, tactile and proximity stimuli; record downstream activity without a digital classifier controlling behaviour.
10. **Sensorimotor loop:** begin with motors raised/restrained; test low-speed commands, encoder feedback, current limits and emergency stop before controlled floor tests.
