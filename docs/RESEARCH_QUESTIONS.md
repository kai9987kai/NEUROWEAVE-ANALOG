# Open Research Questions

1. Which circuit topology preserves four-compartment dynamics without excessive component count?
2. How should six soma channels combine while remaining separately observable?
3. Which low-noise, low-power components meet the chosen ranges and time constants?
4. Can analogue STDP achieve repeatable bounded updates across temperature and tolerances?
5. Which physical mechanism provides durable synaptic state without hidden digital memory?
6. What is the best division between local synapse circuits and regional boards?
7. How can global modulation coexist with receptor-specific local transfer functions?
8. What sparse connectivity and fan-out are achievable per neuron?
9. Can photodiode arrays plus analogue processing support useful mobile vision?
10. Which inertial/proximity sensors offer suitable analogue interfaces?
11. How can recurrent plastic networks remain stable and adaptive?
12. What power/thermal budget is realistic at 16, 64, 128 and 512 neurons?
13. When is custom analogue IC integration more practical than discrete modules?
14. How should DSAG be parameterised and compared with a single-soma control?
15. Which tests distinguish physical learning from a fixed reflex?

Link each question to an experiment, decision record or research note when investigated.
