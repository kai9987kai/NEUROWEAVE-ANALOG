# Analogue Sensory Systems

**Status:** Proposed sensor and analogue front-end architecture.

Physical transducers feed analogue conditioning and sensory-channel circuits. Digital image/audio processing must not secretly become the operational perception pipeline.

- **Vision:** begin with photodiode arrays and analogue channels for intensity, direction, contrast and temporal change; investigate spatial sampling and motion-sensitive circuits. Explore focal-plane/event-based sensors later.
- **Audition:** microphone preamplifiers, analogue band-pass channels, envelope detectors and temporal-change circuits; multiple microphones can support directional comparison.
- **Touch:** pressure-sensitive resistive/capacitive sensors, tactile arrays and bumper/contact switches.
- **Proximity:** infrared and ultrasonic transducers with analogue receiver/conditioning stages.
- **Inertial/spatial:** conditioned acceleration and angular-rate signals, orientation-related signals and wheel feedback.
- **Proprioception:** encoder pulse conditioning, motor-current sensing, wheel speed and steering feedback.
- **Environment:** thermistors and other analogue transducers.

For each sensor document calibration, analogue output range, bandwidth, noise, saturation, latency, temperature/supply sensitivity, failure state and connectome destination. Demonstrate downstream processing in the analogue connectome rather than an external digital classifier.
