# Status, Evidence and Validation Rules

Use exact maturity labels:

- **Proposed:** concept documented, not verified.
- **Modelled:** circuit simulation exists and is archived.
- **Bench-tested:** physical circuit tested with a reproducible protocol.
- **Integrated:** tested with connected modules or on the robot.
- **Validated:** acceptance criteria passed and result is repeatable.

Do not label a concept built, working, learning or autonomous without evidence.

## Minimum experiment evidence

Record experiment ID/date/author, hardware and schematic/BOM revisions, equipment/calibration, supply voltages/current limits, environment/temperature, stimulus waveform/procedure, raw data, predefined acceptance criteria, observed result/uncertainty/failed runs, scope captures/photos, safety anomalies, conclusion and limitations.

## Metrics

Measure applicable offset, gain, time constant, bandwidth, noise, drift, spike amplitude/width, refractory duration, synaptic update curve, retention, power, thermal rise, cross-talk, fan-out loading, network stability and motor stopping distance.

Archive instrument settings, source files, substitutions and fixtures. A result is not repeatable if another researcher cannot reconstruct it.
